# Slabbed Brand Guide

## Positioning

Core promise: **A visual home for vintage card collectors.**

Primary tagline: **Vintage card collecting, built for the modern collector.**

Supporting line: **Browse fronts and backs, track what you own and want, and share a shelf around the cards you love.**

Slabbed should feel like a quiet, visual collector space: card-first, historically aware, useful for tracking, and social without becoming noisy.

## Voice

- Write short.
- Let card images carry the page.
- Use collector-native words: cards, backs, sets, runs, shelf, watchlist, collection, showcase, activity.
- Make features concrete: browse, flip, track, share, add, watch, favorite.
- Keep social language subtle unless real thresholded activity is powering the UI.

## Vocabulary

- **Set** means an official checklist: T206 White Border, T205 Gold Border, 1952 Topps, 1948 Bowman.
- **Run** means a collector chase inside or across sets: Hall Pass, Southern Charm, What a Catch!, team runs, rookie runs, back runs.
- **Shelf** means the visual profile where a collector shows cards, progress, and favorites.
- **Watchlist** means cards someone wants to revisit or chase.
- **Showcase** means the special display area on a collector profile.

## Page Copy Patterns

- Home: lead with the visual collector home, fronts/backs, tracking, and shelf.
- Discover: "Search and browse vintage baseball cards."
- Sets: "Browse vintage checklists."
- Search: "Find players, teams, sets, card numbers, and tobacco backs."
- Profile: display-case language, not dashboard language.
- Collection and Watchlist: direct utility copy with minimal explanation.
- Settings: "Profile, privacy, and card display."

## CTA Rules

- Prefer direct labels: Browse Cards, View Demo Shelf, Create Account, Explore Set, View All.
- Avoid clever labels for structural actions.
- Use playful names for runs only when the run itself is collector-native.

## Avoid

- AI or startup phrasing: unlock, curated collector rails, visual collector profile, prestige-first, rabbit holes, built for collectors like you.
- Repeating "spreadsheet" beyond the homepage positioning.
- Claims of live popularity unless thresholded real social signals are active.
- Long subtitles under every module.
- Explaining obvious UI when cards can do the work.

## Before / After

- Before: "Unlock curated collector rails built for collectors like you."
- After: "Find a chase."

- Before: "Build a visual collector profile with gallery-wall energy."
- After: "Share a shelf around the cards you love."

- Before: "Prestige-first browse for collector rabbit holes."
- After: "Browse by player, set, back, or run."
